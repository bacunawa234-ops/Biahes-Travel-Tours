Good to know
  • Dec–Jan is dry season in Palawan, Boracay, Cebu and Bohol, but tours may be moved or cancelled because of weather, for your safety.
  • Cebu City Tour needs at least 4 guests to depart. If fewer, upgrade to a private tour for +$14.24/person.
  • Christmas and New Year are peak season. Hotels in El Nido and Boracay fill up quickly, so we recommend confirming early.
  • Hotel check-in 2:00–3:00 PM · check-out 12:00 NN. Most hotels ask for a refundable security deposit.
