BIAHES — OFFICIAL LOGO & BRAND COLORS
=====================================

Official logo: blue "B" with a red swoosh and an airplane taking off,
plus a suitcase and a cruise ship inside the lower part of the B.

USAGE TIPS
----------
- Use the logo on white or light backgrounds. On dark photos, place it on a
  small white circle or white box so the ship and suitcase stay visible.
- Keep space around the logo (at least the width of the plane).
- Don't stretch, recolor, or add effects to the logo.
- Minimum size: about 120 px tall on screens.
- For large prints (tarpaulin, signage, shirts), have it redrawn as a vector
  file (SVG/AI) first; this PNG is sized for screens and small prints.
